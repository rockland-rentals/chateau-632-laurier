Common questions

What exactly does the rent include?

Every rent is all-inclusive: heat, hydro and water are included, and so are snow removal and lawn care. Your suite's appliances — fridge, stove, dishwasher, microwave, hood fan, washer and dryer — are included too. Parking and storage are available at extra cost; ask the leasing team for current pricing.

How much is rent?

Available suites run from $2,495 to $2,995 per month, all-inclusive. The 1 bedroom + den suites are listed from $2,495/month and the 2 bedroom / 2 bathroom suites at $2,995/month. Studios are fully leased. For the rent on a specific suite, contact the leasing team — prices and availability can change.

When can I move in?

Château 632 opens in November 2026. Suites are being reserved now — 45% are already reserved — and the showroom is open for tours.

Is it really 55+?

Yes — Château 632 is a hotel-inspired adult lifestyle community tailored for residents aged 55+. The leasing team can confirm how the age guideline applies to your household.

Are barrier-free suites available?

Yes — some suites are barrier-free and wheelchair-accessible, and the building has an elevator. Ask the leasing team which accessible suites remain.

Is parking or storage available?

Yes, parking and storage options are available (priced on request), and there's outdoor bicycle parking on site.

Can I bring a pet?

Please ask the leasing team about the current pet policy when you tour — they'll confirm what applies to your suite.

I'm downsizing — can you help sell my current home?

Yes. As the listing notes, the team can assist you every step of the way — from selling your existing home to making your transition into maintenance-free living as seamless as possible. Ask Chantal Benoit (RE/MAX Delta Realty, 343-209-5833) or the leasing team.

Is there an open house?

Yes — an open house is listed for Sunday, November 1, 2026, from 2:00 to 4:00 PM at 632 Laurier Street (per MLS® listing X13219992). Any other day, just book a tour with the leasing team.

How do I reserve a suite?

Call the leasing team at 343-308-0543 or email leasing@chateau632.ca. Tour the showroom, choose your suite type and floor, and they'll walk you through reserving it.
